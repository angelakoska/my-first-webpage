About Me

Hi, I'm Angela! I'm studying Information Technology at Campus. I've always enjoyed technology, exploring what it can do, and understanding how it works. I like playing around with things and figuring them out for myself.

Organization is a big part of my everyday life. Whether I'm sorting loot in a game or arranging papers, books, and trinkets at home, I like creating systems that make sense to me. That same interest draws me to working with data.

I enjoy organizing items in games, and that’s part of what got me interested in working with data.
